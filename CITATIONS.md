# Citations And External Services

Cladistica uses public sequence data and external phylogenetic tools.
Please cite the underlying data, accessions, and software used in manuscripts.

## Sequence Data

- NCBI GenBank / Nucleotide records and NCBI E-utilities.
  Cite the specific GenBank accession.version identifiers used in the final
  dataset, and cite GenBank/NCBI according to journal requirements.
- Sayers, E. W. et al. (2025). GenBank 2025 update. Nucleic Acids Research,
  53(D1), D56-D61. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae1114
- Sayers, E. (2022). A General Introduction to the E-utilities. Entrez
  Programming Utilities Help. NCBI Bookshelf.
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK25497/

## External Software

- MUSCLE:
  Edgar, R. C. (2004). MUSCLE: multiple sequence alignment with high accuracy
  and high throughput. Nucleic Acids Research, 32(5), 1792-1797.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkh340
- IQ-TREE:
  Minh, B. Q. et al. (2020). IQ-TREE 2: New models and efficient methods for
  phylogenetic inference in the genomic era. Molecular Biology and Evolution,
  37(5), 1530-1534. https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msaa015
- ModelFinder:
  Kalyaanamoorthy, S. et al. (2017). ModelFinder: fast model selection for
  accurate phylogenetic estimates. Nature Methods, 14, 587-589.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4285
- MrBayes:
  Ronquist, F. et al. (2012). MrBayes 3.2: Efficient Bayesian phylogenetic
  inference and model choice across a large model space. Systematic Biology,
  61(3), 539-542. https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/sys029
- Biopython:
  Cock, P. J. A. et al. (2009). Biopython: freely available Python tools for
  computational molecular biology and bioinformatics. Bioinformatics, 25(11),
  1422-1423. https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp163
