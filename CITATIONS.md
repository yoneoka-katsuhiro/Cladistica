# Citations And External Services

Cladistica uses public sequence data and external phylogenetic tools.
Please cite the underlying data and software in manuscripts.

- NCBI GenBank and NCBI E-utilities for accession discovery and sequence retrieval.
- MUSCLE for marker-wise multiple sequence alignment.
- IQ-TREE and ModelFinder for model selection and maximum-likelihood inference.
- MrBayes for Bayesian inference.
- Biopython for GenBank parsing and sequence handling.
